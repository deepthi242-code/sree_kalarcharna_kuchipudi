About the Academy

Where tradition meets expression.

Sree Kalaacharan Nrityalayam is dedicated to nurturing the beauty, discipline and expressive language of Indian classical dance.

Led by Guru Deepthi Polagani, the academy provides training in Kuchipudi and semi-classical dance, encouraging students to learn with devotion and perform with confidence.

“

Dance is not only movement; it is a language of expression, tradition and emotion.

— Guru Deepthi
